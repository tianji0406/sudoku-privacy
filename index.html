Privacy Policy

App: Sudoku: Train Your Brain (Android) · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information the app "Sudoku: Train Your Brain" ("the app") uses, and why. In short: the app has no accounts, we do not collect your name, email or any other personal information ourselves, and your game progress stays on your phone. The only data collected comes from the advertising service built into the free version of the app, described below.

1. Who is responsible

The app is published by [TJ Corporation]. For any privacy question or request, contact: [tianji0406@gmail.com].

2. Information that stays on your device

To let you keep playing, the app saves the following only on your phone: puzzles you have solved, best times, experience points (XP), your daily streak, your chosen theme and background, and whether you have tapped "Rate me". This information is never sent to us or to any server run by us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version of the app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the app may collect and use:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address, not GPS)
  • How you interact with ads and the app
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and app performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Paid plans remove ads from the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Consent in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time via "Ad privacy settings" at the bottom of the home screen. If you do not consent, the app still works, but ads may not be shown.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Paid plans are bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms for how Google handles this.

6. Rating the app

The "Rate me" button opens the app's page on Google Play. Anything you write there is handled by Google Play.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third party involved is Google (advertising and purchases), as described above.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have rights under data-protection law (such as access, correction, deletion and objection), which you can exercise with Google for the data it processes, or by contacting us at the address above with any question.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
